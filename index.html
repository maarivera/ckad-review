How to use this page

  1. Set your exam date above, then work through the Study Plan day by day and tick tasks off as you finish them. Progress is saved in your browser.
  2. Do every lab on a real cluster. Your kubeadm two-node lab, kind, or minikube all work. Reading alone won't get you through the CKA.
  3. Run the Exam Simulator under real conditions: 2-hour timer, the docs as your only reference, solving each task in your own cluster. After that, grade yourself honestly against the checklist.
  4. Use the Quick Quiz to fill concept gaps, and keep the Cheat Sheet open during labs until the commands stick.
  5. Registering for the exam gives you 2 killer.sh sessions (36 hours each). Save them for week 6, once you're scoring 75% or more here.

Exam Simulator

Mock exams in killer.sh style. Solve each task in your own cluster (create the namespaces and objects the task assumes), then grade yourself against the solution checklist.

Rules (same as the real exam)

  • 120 minutes. The timer keeps running if you reload or close the page.
  • Each task shows a weight. Points are awarded per completed checklist item, and you need 66% to pass.
  • Always run the kubectl config use-context command shown on each task first. On the real exam you'll usually ssh into a different host for each task.
  • Skip hard tasks fast: flag them and come back. Low-weight tasks are just as valuable per minute.
  • Allowed resources: kubernetes.io/docs, kubernetes.io/blog, helm.sh/docs, and gateway-api.sigs.k8s.io.
